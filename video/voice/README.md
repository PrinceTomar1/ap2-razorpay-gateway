# Put your nine — sorry, ten — recordings here

One file per slide, named `00` to `09`. Any format: `.m4a`, `.mp3`, `.wav`.

```
video/voice/00.m4a     intro — who you are
video/voice/01.m4a     the problem
video/voice/02.m4a     mandates
video/voice/03.m4a     architecture
video/voice/04.m4a     no LLM on the money path
video/voice/05.m4a     the demo
video/voice/06.m4a     two orders, one capture
video/voice/07.m4a     the audit chain
video/voice/08.m4a     the measured result
video/voice/09.m4a     honest limits
```

The words are in `../../SPEAKING_SCRIPT.md`.

**Timing does not matter.** The build measures each of your clips and holds its
slide for exactly that long. Speak at whatever pace is natural — slower is
usually better.

**Record them separately.** If you fluff a line you redo one slide, not the
whole take. Voice Memos on your Mac or phone is enough.

When all ten are here, say so and the video is rebuilt with your voice.
