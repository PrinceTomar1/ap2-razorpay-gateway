# SPEAKING SCRIPT — RECORD ONE FILE PER SLIDE

TEN CLIPS. SAVE THEM IN `video/voice/` AS `00` THROUGH `09`.
ANY FORMAT — `.m4a`, `.mp3`, `.wav`. VOICE MEMOS IS ENOUGH.

**TIMING DOES NOT MATTER.** THE VIDEO IS REBUILT AROUND YOUR ACTUAL LENGTHS.
THE SECONDS BELOW ARE ONLY A GUIDE TO PACE.

> A NORMAL-CASE VERSION OF THE SAME WORDS IS IN `video/narration.py` —
> ALL-CAPS IS HARDER TO READ ALOUD, SO USE THAT ONE ON YOUR SCREEN.

**ABOUT 30 SECONDS.**

## `02` — MANDATES

**ABOUT 28 SECONDS.**

> A.P. TWO REPLACES THE CREDENTIAL WITH A MANDATE. A SIGNED, CONSTRAINED,
> VERIFIABLE STATEMENT OF WHAT THE BUYER ACTUALLY AUTHORISED. THE BUYER SIGNS
> ONE OPEN MANDATE: FIVE THOUSAND RUPEES A DAY, FIFTEEN HUNDRED PER PURCHASE,
> THREE MERCHANTS, TWENTY FOUR HOURS, BOUND TO THIS AGENT'S KEY. EVERY
> PURCHASE IS THEN A CLOSED MANDATE SIGNED UNDER IT: THIS MUCH, TO THEM, FOR
> THIS EXACT CART, NOW. SO THE AGENT CAN SHOP, AND IT CANNOT PAY. IT HOLDS A
> KEYPAIR AND NO MONEY.

**DELIVERY:** "THE AGENT CAN SHOP, AND IT CANNOT PAY" IS THE LINE PEOPLE REMEMBER. LAND IT.

**ABOUT 39 SECONDS.**

> HERE IS THE SHAPE. THE AGENT TALKS TO THE MERCHANT OVER M.C.P. THE MERCHANT
> SIGNS THE CART. THEN THE VERIFIER RUNS FOURTEEN CHECKS BEFORE ANYTHING
> REACHES RAZORPAY. SIGNATURE. EXACT V.C.T. CLAIM. KEY BINDING, SO A LEAKED

**ABOUT 37 SECONDS.**

> THE LOAD BEARING DECISION IS WHERE I DID NOT PUT A MODEL. THE SPECIFICATION

> A CLASSIFIER OVER A SMALL, FULLY SPECIFIED DOMAIN. DOES THIS SIGNATURE CHECK

**DELIVERY:** THIS IS THE SLIDE THAT WINS "AI JUDGMENT". STRESS THE WORD **NOT**.

**ABOUT 28 SECONDS.**

> THIS IS THE PART I WOULD LOOK AT IF I WERE JUDGING. ATTEMPT FOUR CREATED TWO

## `07` — THE AUDIT CHAIN

**ABOUT 30 SECONDS.**

> EVERY MONEY ACTION WRITES ONE AUDIT ROW, WITH A SENTENCE A PERSON CAN READ.
> THE ROWS ARE HASH CHAINED, AND THE EXPLANATION IS INSIDE THE HASH, NOT
> BESIDE IT. AN AUDIT TRAIL WHERE THE NUMBERS ARE TAMPER EVIDENT BUT THE PROSE
> IS FREELY EDITABLE IS NOT MUCH OF AN AUDIT TRAIL. THE TESTS BREAK THAT CHAIN
> SIX WAYS, AND ALL SIX ARE CAUGHT WITH THE DAMAGED ROW NAMED. THEY DROP THE
> DATABASE TRIGGERS FIRST — BECAUSE A TAMPER EVIDENCE CLAIM YOU HAVE NOT TRIED
> TO BREAK IS A HOPE, NOT A PROPERTY.

**DELIVERY:** THE LAST SENTENCE IS THE BEST LINE IN THE SCRIPT. DON'T RUSH IT.

# HOW TO RECORD

1. **QUIET ROOM.** PHONE ON SILENT.
2. **OPEN VOICE MEMOS.** ALREADY ON YOUR MAC AND PHONE.
3. **RECORD TEN SEPARATE CLIPS.** ONE PER SLIDE.
4. **FLUFFED A LINE? REDO THAT SLIDE ONLY.** THAT IS THE WHOLE POINT OF TEN FILES.
5. **DROP THEM IN** `video/voice/` **NAMED** `00` **TO** `09`**, THEN SAY SO.**

THE VIDEO IS REBUILT WITH YOUR VOICE IN ABOUT TWO MINUTES.
